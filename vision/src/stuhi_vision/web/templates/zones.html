Where is the doorframe?

Drag a rectangle over the doorframe — the part of the picture a person covers as they pass through, rather than the opening they are seen through. Somebody who never overlaps it is treated as background and ignored.

Only the doorway-facing camera sees the doorframe, so it is the only one that needs a zone. The room-facing camera is judged by how a person's size changes instead; drawing a zone on it does nothing except start watching that view for movement too.

Saving also stores this frame as the reference for that camera, so you are told if it gets knocked and the zone needs drawing again. Back to labelling
